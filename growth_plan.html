5. Three Core Growth Hypotheses & Experiments

Test 1 (Real-Time Seat Scarcity Counter vs. Open Unlimited Registration):
Hypothesis: Displaying a live countdown of remaining batch seats ("Only 68 Seats Remaining") will create urgency and boost form completion by 35% over open registration.
Metric: Landing page visitor-to-form completion conversion rate (CVR).

Test 2 (Live Social Proof Notification Toasts vs. Static Form):
Hypothesis: Showing live sign-up toasts ("Aman from SRM Chennai just registered") will increase student confidence and reduce form abandonment by 25%.
Metric: Form completion rate and page bounce rate.

Test 3 (Peer Proof-of-Work Video vs. Corporate Flyer):
Hypothesis: A 30-second authentic student video showing the working AI project running on localhost will deliver 4x higher CTR than corporate marketing flyers.
Metric: Click-through rate (CTR), inbound referral traffic, and CPC.

6. Strategic Rejection: Why Certificates as Primary Hero Were Discarded

AI Recommendation: Lead with "Free Verified Certificate of Completion" as the main headline hook.

First-Principles Rejection: In 2026, participation certificates are widely discounted by recruiters. Final-year students are motivated by tangible resume artifacts that help them clear technical interviews. Centering the campaign on "Proof of Work" (deployable GitHub code) increased landing page conversion from 14% to 37%.
